📖 How to Play

Objective

Be the first team to reach the target score (14 or 7 points). You play in partnership with North against West & East.

The Deal

Players cut for the first Jack — whoever gets the first Jack dealt to them becomes the first dealer. Each player gets 6 cards. The dealer turns up one card for trump.

Beg or Stand

After seeing your hand and the trump, you can BEG for 3 more cards (making it a 9-card round) or STAND with 6 cards. If you beg, the dealer gets to see 3 extra cards too.

Scoring (Kicking)

Points come from winning tricks that contain:

  • Jack of Trump = 3 points (or Hang Jack value)
  • Six of Trump = 2 points (Standard) or Two of Trump = 2 points (Tobago)
  • Ace of Trump = 1 point
  • Game Point = 1 point (team with most Aces, Kings, Tens in tricks)

Playing Tricks

Must follow suit if led. If you can't follow, you can play any card. Trump beats all other suits. Highest trump wins, or highest of led suit if no trump.

All Fours Sayings
